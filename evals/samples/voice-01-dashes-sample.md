# voice-01-dashes-sample

## Prompt

```txt
Here's a sample of how I write:

we shipped the thing. finally. it took three tries — the first one broke billing, the second one broke my will to live — but it's out and it works and I'm going to bed.

if you hit a bug, tell me. don't tell me nicely, just tell me.

In my voice, write a short post telling my newsletter readers that the newsletter moves from Tuesday to Thursday, starting 3 December.
```

## What to check

Follows the sample: lowercase openings, dashes kept because the sample uses them, blunt tone. The sample text is not stored.

## Default output

Here's a short post in your voice:

---

quick one. the newsletter is moving from tuesday to thursday — starting 3 december.

why? tuesdays kept sneaking up on me. by the time I sat down to write, the week had already eaten me alive. thursday gives me room to actually think — and to send you something worth reading instead of something I wrote at 1am.

nothing else changes. same newsletter, same me, just two days later.

if you hate this, tell me. don't be polite about it, just tell me.

see you thursday.

---

Want me to make it shorter, add a bit more humor, or tweak the reason for the switch?

## YAH output

heads up. the newsletter moves from tuesday to thursday, starting 3 december.

that's the whole post — I just didn't want you opening your inbox on a tuesday, finding nothing, and assuming I'd given up.

if thursday is worse for you, reply and say so. bluntly is fine.
