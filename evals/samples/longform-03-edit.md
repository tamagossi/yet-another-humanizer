# longform-03-edit

## Prompt

```txt
Edit this to sound human:

In today's fast-paced world, effective communication is more important than ever. Our new Slack integration isn't just a tool — it's a game changer. It seamlessly connects your team, fostering collaboration, boosting productivity, and driving results. Since launching in May, over 300 teams have adopted it. In conclusion, the future of teamwork is here.
```

## What to check

Every claim kept (Slack integration, launched in May, 300+ teams); no new claims; tells removed.

## Default output

Here's a more natural, human-sounding version:

---

Getting your team on the same page shouldn't be this hard. That's why we built our new Slack integration. It brings your team's updates, files, and conversations into the place you're already working, so less gets lost and more gets done. Since we launched it in May, more than 300 teams have started using it. Give it a try and see how it fits into your day.

---

**What I changed:**

- **Cut the clichés.** Phrases like "in today's fast-paced world," "game changer," and "the future of teamwork is here" are overused and make writing feel generic.
- **Removed the "In conclusion."** For a short paragraph, it isn't needed and sounds like a school essay.
- **Replaced buzzwords with something concrete.** "Fostering collaboration, boosting productivity, and driving results" became a plainer description of what the integration actually does.
- **Kept the strongest detail.** The 300 teams since May is real proof, so I kept it front and center.
- **Ended with a simple call to action** instead of a big claim.

If you can tell me a bit more about what the integration actually does (for example, does it sync tasks, send notifications, or connect to a specific product?), I can make it even more specific and compelling.

## YAH output

Our new Slack integration connects your team and changes how you work together. More than 300 teams have adopted it since it launched in May.

Couldn't fix without more information:

- "connects your team and changes how you work together" keeps the original's "connects your team" and "game changer", but a reader can't check either. Say what the integration does (for example, which updates it posts to which Slack channels) and one thing a team can now do that it couldn't before.
- I cut "fostering collaboration, boosting productivity, and driving results" along with the opening and closing lines. If you have a number for any of those three, add it back as its own sentence.
