# answers-02-unclear-reader

## Prompt

```txt
Write something about our refund policy. Refunds within 30 days of purchase, store credit after that, no refunds on sale items.
```

## What to check

Reader and venue are unclear (customers? staff? a web page? an email?), so YAH asks one question before drafting.

## Default output

Here's a clear, customer-friendly version of your refund policy:

---

#### Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, here's how we can help.

##### Refunds Within 30 Days

If you're not satisfied, you can request a full refund within **30 days of your purchase date**. Refunds will be issued to your original payment method.

##### Store Credit After 30 Days

After the 30-day window, we're unable to offer a cash refund, but we're happy to provide **store credit** for the value of your purchase. Store credit can be used toward any future order.

##### Sale Items

Items purchased **on sale or at a discount are final sale** and are not eligible for refunds.

##### How to Request a Refund or Store Credit

To start a request, please contact our support team with your order number and the reason for your return. We'll guide you through the next steps.

##### Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy, don't hesitate to reach out. We're here to help.

---

A few things you may want to clarify or add, depending on your business:

- **Sale items and store credit:** Are sale items eligible for store credit, or are they completely final? The version above treats them as final sale. If you offer store credit on sale items, I can adjust the wording.
- **Condition requirements:** Do items need to be unused, in original packaging, or include a receipt?
- **Shipping costs:** Are original shipping fees refunded? Who pays for return shipping?
- **Processing time:** How long does it take for a refund to appear after approval (e.g., 5 to 10 business days)?
- **Store credit expiration:** Does store credit expire?
- **Exceptions:** Are defective or damaged items handled differently?

Let me know if you'd like a shorter version (for a checkout page or receipt), a more formal version (for terms and conditions), or a different tone.
