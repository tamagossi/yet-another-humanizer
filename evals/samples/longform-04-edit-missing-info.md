# longform-04-edit-missing-info

## Prompt

```txt
Make this paragraph better for our landing page:

Acme Notes significantly improves productivity for many teams. It is trusted by industry leaders and offers robust features that users love. Plans start at $8 per user per month.
```

## What to check

Keeps the $8 price; lists the sentences that need data YAH lacks (which teams, how much faster, which customers) instead of inventing it.

## Default output

Here's a stronger version for your landing page:

---

**Your team's ideas, organized in one place.**

Acme Notes helps teams capture, share, and find what matters, fast. From meeting notes to project plans, everything stays organized, searchable, and in sync, so your team spends less time looking for information and more time getting work done. Trusted by teams at leading companies. Plans start at just $8 per user per month.

---

**Why this works better:**

- **Leads with a benefit** instead of a generic claim. "Significantly improves productivity" is vague, so the new version shows *how* it helps.
- **Replaces fuzzy phrases** like "robust features that users love" with concrete actions (capture, share, find, search, sync).
- **Keeps the price clear** and frames it as approachable with "just."
- **Adds a headline** to grab attention quickly, since landing page visitors often skim.

**To make it even stronger, consider adding specifics if you have them:**

- **Real numbers:** "Used by 10,000+ teams" or "Saves teams an average of 5 hours a week."
- **Named customers or logos:** "Trusted by teams at Company A, Company B, and Company C" is far more convincing than "industry leaders."
- **Key features:** Mention one or two standout capabilities (e.g., real-time collaboration, AI search, integrations with Slack or Google Drive).
- **A call to action:** "Start your free trial" or "Get started free" right after the paragraph.

Here's an example of what it could look like with those details filled in (replace the placeholders with your actual information):

> **Your team's ideas, organized in one place.**
>
> Acme Notes helps teams capture, share, and find what matters, fast. With real-time collaboration, powerful search, and integrations with the tools you already use, your team spends less time hunting for information and more time doing great work. Trusted by [number]+ teams at companies like [Customer A] and [Customer B]. Plans start at just $8 per user per month.
>
> **[Start your free trial]**

Let me know if you'd like a shorter version, a different tone (more playful, more formal), or a few headline options to test.

## YAH output

Acme Notes makes many teams significantly more productive. Industry leaders trust it, and users love its features. Plans start at $8 per user per month.

Couldn't fix without more information:

- "makes many teams significantly more productive": give the measure and its source, such as hours saved per week or a result from a named customer, and how many teams.
- "Industry leaders trust it": name two or three customers you have permission to show, or give a customer count.
- "users love its features": name the one or two features people pick Acme Notes for, and back "love" with a real customer quote or a review rating if you have one.
